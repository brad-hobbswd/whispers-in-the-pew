Faith & Trust

Reflections on surrender, fear, perseverance, waiting, and learning to trust Jesus through every season of uncertainty and growth.

← Return to Contents

Faith grows where certainty ends.

Faith rarely grows in comfort. Trust is often formed in silence, uncertainty, waiting, and the places where answers feel delayed.

These reflections walk through surrender, perseverance, fear, healing, and learning to recognize the steady voice of Jesus even when the path ahead feels unclear.

“Faith is not the absence of fear, but the decision to trust Jesus in the middle of uncertainty.”
