Courage & Calling

Reflections on courage, purpose, surrender, perseverance, obedience, and boldly following Jesus through every season.

← Return to Contents

Calling often begins where comfort ends.

Courage is not the absence of fear. Courage grows when faith chooses obedience even while uncertainty still whispers in the background. Purpose often requires stepping forward before every answer becomes visible.

These reflections explore surrender, perseverance, identity, boldness, spiritual purpose, and learning to trust Jesus fully in the places where calling feels costly.

“God rarely calls people toward comfort first. He calls them toward trust.”
