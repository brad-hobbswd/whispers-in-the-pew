Section Five

Courage & Calling

Reflections on courage, purpose, perseverance, obedience, and following the voice of Jesus through every season.

← Return to Contents

Faith often grows strongest in uncertain places.

Calling rarely arrives wrapped in comfort. God often speaks through stretching seasons, difficult obedience, quiet endurance, and moments requiring courage beyond natural strength.

These reflections explore perseverance, surrender, boldness, purpose, trust, and learning how to walk faithfully wherever Jesus leads.

“Courage is not the absence of fear. Courage is obedience while trusting God completely.”
