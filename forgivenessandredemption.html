Mercy reaches where pain once lived.

Forgiveness is rarely easy, and redemption does not always arrive quickly. Some wounds remain tender long after the moment passed, and some burdens feel impossible to release.

These reflections walk through mercy, surrender, healing, freedom, and the transforming grace of Jesus that restores hearts weighed down by pain, betrayal, shame, and regret.
