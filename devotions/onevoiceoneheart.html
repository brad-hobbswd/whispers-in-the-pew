Integrity

One Voice, One Heart

Psalm 19:14

← Return to Christian Living

“Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O Lord, my firm, impenetrable Rock and my Redeemer.”

Words Reveal the Heart

Words have incredible power. They can encourage or discourage, heal or wound, reveal truth or conceal it. Every conversation plants something in the heart of the listener. Jesus taught that what comes out of our mouths flows from what is already within us. Our speech often becomes a mirror of our spiritual condition.

As followers of Christ, our words should consistently reflect His grace and truth. Whether we are speaking to family, coworkers, strangers, or fellow believers, our speech should point people toward Him.

The Same Everywhere

It is easy to adjust our language depending on who surrounds us. We may sound different at church than we do at work or with friends. Yet God is not looking for different versions of us. He desires hearts that remain faithful in every setting.

Integrity means our private conversations and our public conversations come from the same surrendered heart. Authentic faith does not perform for an audience. It remains genuine wherever we are.

A heart surrendered to Christ produces a voice that honors Him wherever it is heard.

Growing in Grace

If gossip, harsh words, complaining, or profanity have become familiar companions, God has not abandoned you. Instead, He lovingly calls you into transformation. As He changes the heart, He also changes the tongue.

Every conversation becomes another opportunity to reflect Christ. One voice. One heart. One Savior.

Prayer

Lord, let my words always reflect the work You are doing in my heart. Remove anything that dishonors You and replace it with grace, wisdom, compassion, and truth. Help me to live with integrity so my private life and public life both point to You. Let my speech encourage others and glorify Your holy name. In Jesus' name, amen.
