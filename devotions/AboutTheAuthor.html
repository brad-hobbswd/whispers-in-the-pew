About the Author

A journey through faith, healing, teaching, restoration, and the quiet voice of Jesus through every season of life.

← Return Home

Bradley Hobbs is a devoted follower of Jesus whose journey has been shaped through seasons of silence, heartbreak, searching, restoration, and deep spiritual reflection. Through personal trials, church hurt, healing, and surrender, he discovered the importance of listening closely for the voice of God even within life’s most painful moments.

His heart for writing began through the desire to encourage people who feel overlooked, silenced, uncertain, wounded, or spiritually exhausted. Whispers in the Pew was born from that place of honesty and restoration, offering devotional reflections centered on healing, grace, truth, conviction, hope, and the quiet nearness of Jesus.

“God often speaks most clearly within the places people try hardest to hide.”

Bradley’s passion for helping others extends beyond writing. He holds a degree in Computer Information Technology and Business Management and continues pursuing graduate studies in education with specialization in Preschool through 5th Grade along with a Director’s Credential in Early Childhood settings.

His son Gabriel became one of the greatest inspirations behind both teaching and writing. Watching Gabriel grow encouraged Bradley to begin working with children professionally, first within Early Childhood Development and later within the local school district where he cared for and taught young learners with patience, compassion, and encouragement.

Life experiences involving heartbreak, toxic environments, personal struggles, and spiritual rebuilding deeply shaped the themes found throughout his devotional work. Rather than allowing pain to silence purpose, Bradley chose to bring those experiences before God and allow healing to become part of the message shared with others.

Alongside Whispers in the Pew, Bradley continues developing additional devotional projects, children’s books, reflective writings, and more personal works centered around spiritual healing, identity, grace, and growth through Jesus.

His prayer remains simple:

That every reader would discover that Jesus still heals, still restores, still speaks, and still walks beside people through every hidden place of life.
