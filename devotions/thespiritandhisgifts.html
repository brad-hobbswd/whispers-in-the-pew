Holy Spirit

The Spirit and His Gifts

Living Within, Working Through

← Return to Holy Spirit

“Now there are distinctive varieties of spiritual gifts, but it is the same Spirit Who is the source of them all.”

1 Corinthians 12:4 AMPC

“Do you not discern and understand that you are God's temple (His sanctuary), and that God's Spirit has His permanent dwelling in you?”

1 Corinthians 3:16 AMPC

The Holy Spirit is God's greatest gift to every believer. He is not merely a feeling during worship or a moment of inspiration. He is the very presence of God dwelling within those who belong to Him. His presence changes everything. He comforts, teaches, convicts, strengthens, and leads us into truth each day.

The Greatest Gift

The greatest gift God has given His Church is not a talent or an ability. It is His Spirit. When the Holy Spirit fills a believer, God Himself comes to dwell within them. We become His temple, set apart for His glory and marked as His own.

His presence is not reserved for church services or special moments. He walks with us at home, at work, in seasons of joy, and through times of sorrow. Wherever we go, His Spirit goes with us.

The Gifts Flow Through Us

The Holy Spirit does not simply live within us. He also ministers through us. Scripture teaches that He distributes spiritual gifts according to His will. Some receive words of wisdom, others words of knowledge, healing, prophecy, discernment, tongues, interpretation, faith, or other gifts that strengthen the Body of Christ.

These gifts are not the Holy Spirit Himself. They are the evidence of His work flowing through surrendered lives. The Spirit is the Person. The gifts are His expressions. They exist to glorify Jesus, encourage believers, and reach those who need Him.

The Spirit is God's presence within us. His gifts are God's power flowing through us.

The Difference Matters

Understanding this difference protects us from pursuing gifts while neglecting the Giver. Spiritual gifts are wonderful, but intimacy with the Holy Spirit is greater. Gifts may bless others, but His presence transforms us from the inside out.

When our relationship with Him comes first, the gifts find their proper place. They become instruments of love instead of opportunities for recognition. Everything He does points people to Jesus Christ.

Story Time

The Quiet Prayer and the Bold Word

Anthony was a young believer who loved God deeply. He knew the Holy Spirit lived within him, yet he often wondered why others seemed to operate in spiritual gifts while he quietly served in the background.

One evening during a small group gathering, the leader invited anyone needing prayer to come forward. A woman began to cry as she shared how forgotten and invisible she felt. As everyone listened, Anthony sensed a quiet thought rise within his heart.

"Tell her I see her."

His heart raced. Was that simply his own compassion, or was the Holy Spirit speaking? Anthony bowed his head and whispered, "Lord, if this is You, help me to obey."

With trembling hands and a shaking voice, he gently spoke.

"Sister, I believe the Lord wants you to know that He sees you. He has not forgotten you."

The woman covered her face and wept even more. Through tears she replied, "Before I came tonight, that was exactly what I asked God to tell me."

Anthony walked home with a new understanding. The Holy Spirit had already been living inside him all along. That evening, the Spirit simply chose to work through him to encourage someone else.

The Spirit remained the same Person living within him. The gift was simply one beautiful expression of His presence.

Prayer

Holy Spirit, thank You for making Your home within me. Help me never to value the gifts You give more than Your presence. Teach me to walk with You daily, to listen for Your voice, and to remain surrendered to Your leading. Use my life as a vessel through which Your love, grace, wisdom, and power may flow to others. May everything You do through me bring honor and glory to Jesus Christ. In Jesus' name, amen.
